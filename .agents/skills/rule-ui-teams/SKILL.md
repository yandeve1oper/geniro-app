# Teams

- "Team" is the thing; the noun rule is in the root `AGENTS.md`. The renderer screen is `teams/`, never a canvas: `Teams.tsx` holds the state (load, autosave, chat dock, dialogs) and draws `teams-library.tsx` (cards with a portrait of members, `team-card.tsx`) or `team-page.tsx` (header, `team-hero.tsx`, Team / Memory / Statistics tabs over `components/tab-strip.tsx`). The Team tab is Members (`member-card.tsx`, opening `member-sheet.tsx` beside the page — name, avatar, character, role, instructions, the agent settings, MCP, pool and removal; full screen on a phone; the last member cannot be removed), Stages (the mode, `stage-journey.tsx` led by its `EntryCard` — the trigger's member, required — and `stages-dialog.tsx`) and Team instructions (`instructions-card.tsx`: blocks with an audience through `member-pick.tsx` `AudiencePick`). Every reading is the daemon's: the hero's figures, the Statistics tab (its **How each member works** table included) and the member sheet's **Performance** group (`member-performance.tsx`: the member's figures and their spread per run, every change that touched it with the effect the daemon measured, and every analysis's marks of it, newest first, with the judge and the user's own verdict — `components/assessment-verdict.tsx`, also on each member row of the recommendations card through `MemberVerdictsContext`; marks and verdicts drawn by `components/member-marks.tsx` everywhere) are `GET /v1/stats/teams/:slug` (`use-team-stats.ts`), the Memory tab lists the team's entries (`use-team-memories.ts`, scope `team`), and the warning pill, status dots and the member sheet's pinned problems merge the editor's own refusals with `GET /v1/teams/:slug/health` (`use-team-health.ts`, read again after every save) and Electron main's sign-in reading (`use-sign-in-health.ts`, `detectClis({maxAgeMs})`, never a probe per page open; asked afresh after a sign-in settles). Only the open team's page asks `probe=true` (it lists each profile's MCP servers once, which starts the user's stdio servers, and re-reads while a member's `checking` is set). The library's **⚠ N** is the daemon's cheap reading (`probe=false`, at most four teams at once) plus each member seat's sign-in from the same cached `detectClis({maxAgeMs})` (`MemberHealth.seats`), labelled with the age of the MCP reading; it does not count the editor's refusals, which need the team open. Deleting a team asks what happens to its memories when it keeps any (`team-memory-fate.tsx`, `DELETE /v1/teams/:slug?memories=forget|keep|global`). A fix button per problem kind is `use-member-fixes.tsx`; only a red problem stops a run (the daemon's `TEAM_NOT_READY`).
- The page's **⚠ N need setup** pill (`warning-pill.tsx`) lists distinct problems, not member findings: `groupMemberFindings` (`team-problems.ts`) folds a finding about one CLI account (agent + profile) into one row naming every member it reaches, MCP servers counted and listed on request (`details`), with one fix — so a profile's servers across five members are one row, not a row per server per member. Errors come first.
- A member card's setup problems are one **!** badge in its corner (`member-card.tsx`, a `HoverPopover` listing each problem with its fix). A member's face is `components/member-avatar.tsx` (`MemberAvatar`, `AvatarStack`), everywhere: picture, emoji or initials on `bg-avatar-*`, falling back to initials when a picture cannot be read. A picture is uploaded by `components/avatar-picker.tsx` (square-cropped on a canvas, PNG) to `POST /v1/teams/avatars` and read back as a `data:` URI through `AvatarImageLoaderContext` (provided by `App`), since the CSP allows no other image source. Transcript identity reads the run's snapshot (`TranscriptNodeMeta.avatar`), never the library, and never shows the character.
- The team document's edits are pure functions in `teams/team-doc.ts` (`withMember`, `withoutMember`, `withTriggerMember`, `teamFromMeta`). Removing a member also drops it from every block audience and stage `responsible` list, and hands the trigger to the first member left. A new team starts with one member that handles the first message, so it saves at once.
- `teams/team-validate.ts` (twin of the daemon's `validateTeam`), `member-validate.ts` (field limits and NUL rules, twin of `teams.types.ts`) and `stage-validate.ts` (twin of `validateStages`) mirror every daemon refusal; while any refusal stands, autosave holds and the status bar names it, since one refused field would stop every later save. A warning (an audience or `responsible` id naming no member) never holds a save.
- Member and block derivations over the generated types live in `teams/member-schema.ts` (`runsUnasked`, `audienceOf`, `audienceReaches`, `modelChangePatch`); `audienceOf` exists because the generator renders the audience union as an empty interface.
- **Versions…** (the open team's overflow menu, only with a saved team): `teams/team-versions-dialog.tsx` over `use-team-versions.ts`, read only while open and again after `usage_recorded`. Every figure is the daemon's (`GET /v1/stats/team-versions`); the dialog never folds or sums one. It is a summary strip (runs, versions, average cost per run) over one card per version, newest first, not sortable: `#n` and hash with its badges, dates used and runs, average cost per run, and `DiffFigures` (`+N −M`); one layout on desktop and phone. An unmeasured figure or a missing diff says why as visible text, never hover-only. A press on a numbered version replaces the list with that version's `git diff` against the one numbered before it (`chats/diff-view.tsx` `UnifiedDiff`); there are no expanding rows and no run list (user decision). That view also restores the version (`ConfirmActionButton`, never for the current one): the Teams screen flushes autosave, posts the restore quoting `loadedHashRef`, then re-opens the team from disk (`openTeam`) and names the backup in the status bar; a version recorded before members is labelled "before teams"; a `TEAM_CHANGED` refusal raises the stale-copy banner. It is held back, with the reason shown, while the builder chat's agent works or the editor's copy is stale. The `current` mark is the library team's; a team not run since its last edit is its own first card ("New version", "not run yet"), and it opens to its diff but offers no restore.
- Library and member cards take activation from `NavListItem`. Never use a `div role="button"` with a nested delete or fix button. A library card counts members and stages, never nodes or edges.
- `Teams.openRequest` (a chat card's "Open team") leaves an open team the way Back does: flushed first, and the "Leave without saving?" prompt when that fails, opening the request on Discard.
- Never pair `line-clamp-*` with a `display` utility.
- A block runs nothing and has no `node_state`.
- Member fields that reach argv, and a stage's text, refuse NUL only, because the same schema reads saved YAML.
- Switching a member's CLI starts from that CLI's defaults; the editor remembers the last CLI picked for a new member (`rememberMemberAgent`).
- `useAgentVocabulary` resolves during render off its cache, never from effect-set state.
- Free-text model mode waits for a non-empty list.
- There is no model adopt and no effort adopt, because unset is a real answer.
- The member sheet uses the composer's chips, never builder-only copies. Approval modes come from `approvals[]` with `plan` filtered out.
- The builder's MCP read is not gated on a press: its 4xx is the only signal of a bad config path.
- A member's MCP switches are its own `mcpDisabled` and write nothing the CLI keeps.
  - A switched-off name the profile does not list shows as an `off` row.
  - Switching CLI clears the list.
  - Pool members fetch under their own CLI and profile.
- Instruction sizes (`useInstructionSizes`) are measured while the Team tab or a selected member is on view, never on the Memory or Statistics tab with no one selected. The Team tab draws each block's own size on its card (`InstructionSizesWire.blocks`: its trimmed text and how many members carry it) and every member's total under the blocks (`team-instruction-sizes.tsx`); the member sheet keeps that member's breakdown.

- **Project overrides** live on the project's page, never the team's (user decision): the team page shows only **Used by** (`team-used-by.tsx`, the projects whose default it is, each opening its page) and, in the delete confirm, whose overrides go with it. The project page's Team section (`projects/project-team-overrides.tsx` over `use-team-overlay.ts` and `team-overlay-doc.ts` — twin of the daemon's `applyTeamOverlay` for the Model group) draws the team as that project runs it and writes only the overlay, autosaved quoting its hash (`TEAM_OVERLAY_CHANGED` keeps the edits and offers Reload): member cards with their override badges, the member sheet in project mode (name and avatar a `fieldset disabled`, the team's character, role and instructions as `ReadOnlyText`, Model rows `MemberModelGroup` — shared with the team page — with `scopeOf`, i.e. `OverridableRow`s whose controls stay live: changing one overrides it for the project, which marks the row with an accent edge, the project's name and a reset back to the team's value, a quiet Team tag otherwise; the pool the same way, overridden whole by its first change; extra instructions), and no copy of the team's instruction blocks: **Manage team** opens the team's page. **Team blocks only in <project>** (`project-scope-instructions.tsx`) adds, edits and removes the overlay's own blocks, each with an audience; it is not a twin of the project's instructions, which reach every member and name no audience (user decision). Stages are the team's and take no project addendum (user decision), and the sheet in project mode shows the Tools & MCP group as the member runs in the project (`MemberMcpRow`, shared with the team page: the listing of its effective agent and config directory, sign-in and Add/Edit JSON writing that profile); its switches are an override like the pool, overridden whole by the first switch (`members.<id>.mcpDisabled`, `[]` = none off) with the same edge, name and reset. The sheet ends in **Restore team settings** (`withTeamSettings`: every Model setting, the pool and the MCP switches back to the team's, the extra instructions kept; behind a confirm). An overlay's `allMembers` (one default per CLI) has no editor (user decision: the panel was unclear); the project's chat sets it, and every reading applies it. The sheet is portalled into the page's side slot (`project-page-slots.ts`).
- **The project's chat** is opened from **Change with chat** in the project page's hero (`project-detail.tsx`), for a project whatever its default, and docked into the page's bottom slot (`ProjectChatPanel` over `chats/hosted-chat-dock.tsx`, the dock `TeamChatPanel` uses too). It is one chat per project (`POST|DELETE /v1/projects/:projectId/chat`), and proposes the project's instruction blocks and its default team's overlay on cards — and, like the team's builder chat (`TeamChatPanel`, opened from the team's page only and bound to no project) and an analysis, any other project's or team's when asked (user decision). Before every send the page writes what is typed and not yet saved (the instructions form's and the overlay's `flushRef`); while a turn works the overlay is not autosaved (`chatWorking`); after each turn the page re-reads the project list and the Team section reads the team and overlay back (`reloadKey`).

**What the builder must never lose** (pinned in `Teams.spec.tsx`):

- Closing the chat dock hides it and never unmounts it. Autosave is paused while the agent works. The dock's turn ending starts the builder's re-read, and the next send waits for it before writing the editor (`reloadAfterChatTurn`, `beforeChatSend`): a queued message leaves as the turn before it ends. A send no turn ended ahead of re-reads nothing, so a file changed elsewhere still meets `TEAM_CHANGED`.
- The post-turn re-read compares against the last load or save (`teamSnapshot`), never against the editor.
- Leaving waits for `flush`. A late save or re-read answer for another team is dropped.
- Every save quotes the `hash` the team was loaded or last saved at. A `TEAM_CHANGED` refusal (an analysis changed the file) holds autosave and offers Reload, which replaces the unsaved edits; nothing is written over the newer file.
- `RunDto.awaiting` follows the three-state rule: absent asserts nothing, and `null` clears.
- Every save writes `normalizedTeam` (`team-doc.ts`): `maxActiveTurns` and the description ride it like every other field, and an empty stage list is written as ABSENT, never `[]`; a save that dropped a field would lose it or loop autosave.
- `maxActiveTurns` is the "Edit team" dialog's agents-at-once row: a switch (off is no limit of its own, the machine ceiling) and a `Stepper` from 1 to the ceiling, so no value outside the range can be entered.
- `stages` (the team's stage graph) is drawn as `StageJourney` on the Team tab and edited in `stages-dialog.tsx`, whose "Responsible" pick is ordered (`ResponsiblePick`; the first member leads). A stage whose `responsible` names a removed member is a warning, never a refusal. Edit stages, a stage card and Add stage open the dialog, saved by autosave. With no stages the section is one dashed row — the first-message member and who runs every job — and the lead mode (One lead / Relay) is shown only once there are stages (or the team is already in relay). The whole team, stages included, is changed by talking through the header's one **Change with chat**; the builder agent edits stages through its ordinary `propose_team` card, which draws the proposed stages and lists each stage change by id (`TeamDiff` `addedStages` / `removedStages` / `changedStages`; a reorder alone is the `stages` team field). `components/stage-graph.tsx` (React Flow + ELK) stays the chat surfaces' stage graph only.
- A pooled member's card in the agents panel shows the slot its own conversation runs on and moves it (`chats/pool-slot-control.tsx`, from the nodes route's `poolMember` / `poolMemberPinned` / `poolMemberNext` through `ownPoolOf`; Chats re-reads the nodes after a pick).
- Agent pools are capped at the twin `MAX_AGENT_POOL_EXTRA_MEMBERS`. Call instances name their pool member (`CallBlockEntry.member`). Hand-offs above the page arrive as anchors. A hand-off row is a card row like any other: drawn where it happened when the card's read pages reach it, else as one note under the pager (`CallBlockEntry.poolHandOff`). Never route it out of the card by `anchorIds`: a team run's call rows all reach the fold as anchors (`structure`).
